Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026 · Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short

  • Your scans, scan history and the QR codes you create stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • We don't require an account and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first. You can change your choice anytime in the app.

This Privacy Policy explains how the QR Safe Scanner Android app (the “App”) handles information. The App is published by Global Design Development (“we”, “us”). By using the App you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the App.

1. Information that stays on your device

The App's main features work entirely on your phone. None of the following information is sent to us.

Camera

The camera is used only to detect QR codes and barcodes while the scanner is open. Camera images are processed in real time on your device and are never saved or uploaded.

Images you choose to scan

When you use Scan From Image, the App reads only the image you pick, on your device, to find codes. The image is not copied, stored or uploaded.

Scan history and favorites

Scanned content, its type, the date and time, and whether you marked it as a favorite are stored in the App's private storage on your phone. You can:

  • delete single items or all history in the App's History screen;
  • turn history off completely in Settings › Save scan history;
  • limit how many items are kept in Settings › Maximum history.

Uninstalling the App deletes all of this data. Phone backups do not include it.

QR codes you create

Text you enter to create a QR code (for example a link, Wi-Fi details or contact details) is used only to draw the code on your device. Images are saved to your phone's Pictures folder only when you tap Save Image.

Settings

Your preferences, such as theme, sounds and security warnings, are stored on your device.

Link safety checks

Before you open a scanned link, the App checks it for warning signs. This check runs on your device; the link is not sent to us or to any other service. The App never opens links or downloads files automatically.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free because it shows ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system version, language and app version);
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped, plus diagnostic and performance data.

Your scans, scan history and the QR codes you create are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the App, a consent message lets you accept or decline personalised ads and data use by Google and its ad partners. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices (ads). If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on general context rather than your profile.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID on your phone. Depending on your Android version, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads. You can also manage ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

4. When information leaves the App because you choose

When you tap Share, Open Link, Connect (Wi-Fi), Call, Send Email or similar actions, the content is passed to the app or website you choose. Their privacy policies apply from that point.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for use by them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us.

6. Data retention and deletion

We don't operate servers that store your data, so we hold no personal information about you. Data on your device stays until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy policy and the ad choices above.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. Because App data stays on your device, you can view and delete it directly in the App. For advertising data, use the ad choices above or contact Google. For any other question, contact us.

8. Security

Scan history is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage is completely secure, but we limit risk by not collecting your data at all.
